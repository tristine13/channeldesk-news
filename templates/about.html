About Us

The Channel Desk.

The Channel is the official tertiary student publication of Fellowship Baptist College. The Channel Desk is its newsroom and its reading room: where our student journalists write, edit, review and publish, and where every student can read the results.

Who we are

The Channel is written and run by students of Fellowship Baptist College, guided by an adviser. Our members work as writers, photojournalists, layout artists and editors, each bringing a different skill to the same goal: telling the stories of our campus well.

By Truth, We Shape Lives.

Our motto is how we work. We aim for accurate, fair reporting, because what a publication prints shapes how a community sees itself.

Looking for a certain kind of read? Browse our Tabloids for quick, sharp stories or our Magazines for long-form reading.

How a story gets published

Nothing appears here until it has been checked. Every work goes through the same four steps.

  1. 1WrittenA staff member writes and submits the work.
  2. 2EditedThe News Editor reviews it first.
  3. 3EndorsedThe Editor-in-Chief gives the next sign-off.
  4. 4ApprovedThe Adviser gives the final approval, then it goes live.
